How to Calculate a Tip

A tip, also known as a gratuity, is an additional payment given for a service. The amount may depend on service quality, local customs, personal preferences and whether a service charge is already included.

Tip Calculation Formula

Tip Amount = Eligible Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

Total Payment = Original Bill + Tax + Tip

Payment Per Person = Total Payment ÷ Number of People

Practical Tipping Guide

Restaurant Dining

In the United States, tips of approximately 15% to 20% are customary in many full-service restaurants. Tipping practices differ by country and establishment.

Food Delivery

When choosing a delivery tip, consider the delivery distance, order size, weather conditions and service provided.

Hotels and Personal Services

Tipping practices for hotel staff, hairdressers, drivers and other service providers vary. Check local expectations before deciding how much to leave.

Automatic Gratuity and Service Charges

Some restaurants automatically add a gratuity or service charge, particularly for larger groups. Review your receipt before adding another tip. A service charge is not necessarily distributed to staff in the same way as a voluntary tip.

Splitting Bills Fairly

Equal bill splitting is convenient when everyone has similar expenses. When individual orders differ significantly, calculate each person's share separately, including their portion of tax and gratuity.

Calculating Tips Mentally

To calculate 10%, move the decimal point one place to the left. Double that amount for a 20% tip. For a 15% tip, add half of the 10% amount to the original 10% amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a 20% tip on $80?

A 20% tip on $80 is $16, making the total $96 before any additional tax.

Should I tip before or after tax?

This depends on local customs and personal preference. Our calculator allows either method.

Can I calculate a custom tip percentage?

Yes. Enter any nonnegative percentage in the tip percentage field.

Can I split a bill without leaving a tip?

Yes. Set the tip percentage to zero and enter the number of people sharing the bill.

What if a service charge is already included?

Review your receipt and ask the establishment whether additional gratuity is expected. Include any existing mandatory charges in your bill amount without counting them twice.

Does the calculator convert currencies?

No. The currency selector changes the currency displayed. For exchange rates, use our Currency Converter.

Why can individual payments differ by a cent?

Some totals cannot be divided equally into whole cents. For example, splitting $100 among three people gives approximately $33.33 each, with one person paying an additional cent to settle the full bill.
