Use this free loan payment calculator to estimate the monthly payment, total amount paid, and total interest for a fixed-rate loan.

How the Loan Payment Calculator Works

The calculator estimates payments for a standard fixed-rate installment loan. Your monthly payment is calculated using the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment period.

Loan Payment Formula

The standard monthly payment formula is:

M = P × [r(1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Where M is the monthly payment, P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Example

For example, a $25,000 loan at a 6.5% annual interest rate for 5 years requires 60 monthly payments. The calculator estimates the monthly payment as well as the total amount and interest paid over the life of the loan.

What Is Included?

This calculator estimates principal and interest for a fixed-rate loan. Actual lender payments may differ because of fees, insurance, taxes, origination charges, late fees, or other costs.

Why Loan Term Matters

A longer loan term generally lowers the monthly payment but may increase the total interest paid. A shorter term generally results in a higher monthly payment but can reduce total interest.
