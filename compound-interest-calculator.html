Use this free compound interest calculator to estimate how an initial amount can grow over time with compound interest and optional monthly contributions.

How Compound Interest Works

Compound interest means that interest can be earned on both the original principal and previously accumulated interest. Over longer periods, compounding can have a substantial effect on the future value of money.

Compound Interest Formula

For the starting amount, the basic formula is:

A = P(1 + r/n)nt

Where P is the starting principal, r is the annual interest rate as a decimal, n is the number of compounding periods per year, and t is time in years.

Recurring Contributions

This calculator also allows monthly contributions. Contributions are added monthly and then participate in subsequent growth. Because contribution timing and compounding frequency can differ among real accounts, the result is an estimate.

Example

If you start with $10,000, contribute $200 each month and earn an average annual return of 7%, the calculator estimates how much the account could grow over 10 years.

Important Considerations

Actual investment returns are not guaranteed and may vary substantially. Taxes, fees, inflation, market losses and other factors are not included unless specifically stated.
