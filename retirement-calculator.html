Use this free retirement calculator to estimate how much your savings could grow by the time you retire based on your current savings, monthly contributions, expected annual return, and time until retirement.

How the Retirement Calculator Works

The calculator estimates the future value of your current retirement savings and monthly contributions. It assumes monthly compounding and that contributions are made at the end of each month.

Years Until Retirement

The number of years available for saving can have a major effect on retirement savings. A longer investment period provides more time for contributions and compound growth.

Example

A 40-year-old planning to retire at age 67 has 27 years until retirement. If that person already has $50,000 saved, contributes $500 each month, and earns an average annual return of 7%, the calculator estimates the potential retirement balance at age 67.

Why Monthly Contributions Matter

Regular contributions can substantially increase retirement savings. Even relatively small increases in monthly contributions may have a meaningful effect when maintained over many years.

Expected Investment Return

The expected annual return is only an assumption. Actual investment returns can vary from year to year and may be positive or negative. No investment return is guaranteed.

What This Calculator Does Not Include

This estimate does not account for inflation, taxes, investment fees, employer matching contributions, Social Security benefits, pensions, withdrawals, or changes in contribution amounts unless those amounts are incorporated separately.
