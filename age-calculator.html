Use this free age calculator to calculate your age in years, months, and days based on your date of birth.

How the Age Calculator Works

The calculator compares your date of birth with the date on which you want your age calculated. It then determines the completed years, months, and days between the two dates.

What Can You Use It For?

You can use the age calculator to determine a person's current age or their age on a specific past or future date. It can be useful for birthdays, forms, records, planning, and other everyday calculations.

Why Age Is More Than a Year Difference

Simply subtracting birth years may give an incorrect result if the birthday has not yet occurred in the year being examined. This calculator takes the month and day into account.

Example

Someone born on January 15, 2000 will not turn 27 until January 15, 2027. Before that date, the person's completed age remains 26 years.
