Use this free savings calculator to estimate how much your savings could grow with an initial deposit, regular monthly contributions, and compound interest.

How the Savings Calculator Works

The calculator begins with your current savings and adds your monthly contributions. Interest is calculated using monthly compounding so you can estimate how your money may grow over time.

What Is Compound Interest?

Compound interest means you can earn interest on your original savings and on interest that has already been added to the account. The effect of compounding generally becomes more noticeable over longer periods.

Example

Suppose you have $5,000 saved and add $300 at the end of each month. If the account earns an annual rate of 4% compounded monthly, this calculator estimates your balance after 10 years.

Why Regular Saving Matters

Regular contributions can make a significant difference over time. Increasing the amount saved each month or saving for a longer period can increase the estimated future balance.

Understanding the Results

The final balance includes your starting savings, monthly contributions, and estimated interest. The calculator also separates the amount you contributed from the estimated interest earned.
