How the Salary Calculator Works

This calculator converts income into equivalent pay periods. It uses your working hours, working days and paid weeks to estimate annual earnings and average income throughout the year.

Hourly to Annual Salary Formula

Annual salary = Hourly pay × Hours per week × Paid weeks per year

For example, someone earning $25 per hour and working 40 hours per week for 52 paid weeks earns an estimated annual gross salary of $52,000.

Annual Salary to Hourly Pay

Divide your annual salary by your total paid working hours. For example, a $60,000 annual salary divided by 2,080 paid hours equals approximately $28.85 per hour.

Biweekly vs. Semimonthly Pay

Biweekly pay normally involves 26 payments per year, while semimonthly pay involves 24 payments per year. These payment schedules produce different paycheck amounts even when annual earnings are identical. Some calendar years may contain an additional biweekly payday.

Gross Pay vs. Net Pay

Gross pay is income before deductions. Net pay is the amount received after applicable income taxes, retirement contributions, insurance premiums and other deductions. This calculator estimates gross pay only.

How Unpaid Leave Affects Income

An hourly employee earning $25 per hour for 40 hours per week would earn $52,000 over 52 paid weeks. With two unpaid weeks, annual earnings would decrease to $50,000, assuming the same hourly rate and weekly hours.

Comparing Job Offers

Salary conversion helps compare hourly and salaried positions. However, compensation also includes benefits, paid leave, overtime, bonuses, retirement contributions and health insurance. Consider these factors alongside gross salary.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many working hours are in a year?

A standard 40-hour workweek over 52 paid weeks equals 2,080 hours. Your actual paid hours may differ.

Does this calculator include overtime?

No. It assumes a constant pay rate. Overtime premiums, bonuses and commissions are not automatically included.

Can part-time workers use this calculator?

Yes. Enter your actual weekly hours and paid working weeks to estimate your income.

Why is monthly pay not simply four times weekly pay?

A year contains approximately 52 weeks but only 12 months. Average monthly income is therefore calculated by dividing annual income by 12 rather than multiplying weekly income by four.

Does changing the currency convert exchange rates?

No. The currency selector changes the displayed currency only. Use our Currency Converter to convert between currencies.
