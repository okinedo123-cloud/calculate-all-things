What Is Sales Tax?

Sales tax is a consumption tax imposed on certain purchases of goods and services. Businesses generally collect applicable sales tax from customers and remit it to the appropriate tax authority.

In the United States, sales tax rates may vary by state, county, city, product category and transaction location. Some purchases may qualify for exemptions or reduced tax rates.

How to Calculate Sales Tax

Sales Tax = Original Price × Tax Rate ÷ 100

Total Price = Original Price + Sales Tax

Worked Example: Adding Sales Tax

Suppose you purchase an appliance for $250 and the applicable sales tax rate is 8%.

How to Remove Sales Tax From a Total

When a receipt shows a tax-inclusive price, dividing the total by one plus the tax rate expressed as a decimal gives the estimated original price.

Original Price = Total Price ÷ (1 + Tax Rate ÷ 100)

Sales Tax = Total Price − Original Price

Worked Example: Reverse Sales Tax

A purchase costs $108 including an 8% sales tax.

DescriptionAmount
Total price$108.00
Tax rate8%
Original price$100.00
Sales tax$8.00

Simply subtracting 8% from $108 would give $99.36, which is incorrect because the tax was originally calculated on the pre-tax price.

How to Find an Effective Sales Tax Rate

When both the original price and the final price are known, you can estimate the effective tax rate.

Effective Tax Rate (%) = (Total Price − Original Price) ÷ Original Price × 100

For example, if a $200 purchase costs $218 after tax, the tax amount is $18 and the effective tax rate is 9%.

This calculation assumes the difference between the two prices consists entirely of tax. Additional fees or charges would affect the result.

Combined State and Local Sales Tax

Some jurisdictions impose more than one sales tax. When state and local taxes apply to the same taxable amount, their percentages can generally be added together.

Practical Applications

Planning a Shopping Budget

If your total budget is $500 and the applicable sales tax rate is 8%, your maximum estimated pre-tax purchase price is $462.96, assuming no other charges. Reverse sales tax calculations help prevent unexpected checkout expenses.

Comparing Purchases

Compare the final price of products, including applicable taxes, delivery charges and other fees. A lower advertised price does not necessarily produce a lower total cost.

Discounts and Sales Tax

Sales tax is often calculated on the discounted purchase price, although the treatment of coupons and rebates depends on local tax rules. Use our Discount Calculator to estimate purchases involving sequential discounts and sales tax.

Business Invoices

Businesses can use this calculator to estimate tax on taxable transactions. However, official accounting and invoicing should follow applicable tax laws, including product exemptions, jurisdiction-specific rates and required rounding methods.

Tax-Inclusive Pricing and VAT

Many countries display prices that already include value-added tax, commonly called VAT. The reverse-tax formula can estimate the pre-tax amount when a single known VAT rate applies. VAT and retail sales tax differ in their collection systems and legal treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is 8% sales tax on $100?

The sales tax is $8, making the total purchase price $108.

How much is 10% sales tax on $250?

The tax is $25, resulting in a total price of $275.

Does every purchase include sales tax?

No. Taxability varies by jurisdiction, product type, customer eligibility and transaction circumstances.

Does this calculator automatically find my local tax rate?

No. Enter the applicable tax rate manually. Consult your state or local tax authority for current official rates.

Can I calculate sales tax on a discounted item?

Yes. Enter the applicable taxable purchase price after discounts, where permitted by local tax rules.

Why might my receipt differ from this calculator?

Retailers may calculate tax separately for each item, apply different tax rates to different products or use jurisdiction-specific rounding rules.

Does changing the currency convert exchange rates?

No. The currency selector changes the displayed currency only. Use our Currency Converter for exchange-rate calculations.
