Use this free adult BMI calculator to estimate your body mass index from your height and weight. BMI is a screening measure and does not diagnose an individual's health or body composition.

How BMI Is Calculated

For measurements in pounds and inches, BMI is calculated by dividing weight in pounds by height in inches squared and multiplying the result by 703.

BMI Formula

BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)2

Adult BMI Categories

For adults, commonly used BMI categories are: underweight below 18.5, healthy weight 18.5 to 24.9, overweight 25.0 to 29.9, and obesity 30.0 or higher.

Example

A person who weighs 180 pounds and is 5 feet 10 inches tall has a BMI of approximately 25.8.

Important Limitations

BMI is a screening measure rather than a direct measurement of body fat. It does not distinguish fat from muscle or account for all differences in age, sex, body composition, pregnancy, or individual health circumstances. Discuss questions about weight or health with an appropriate healthcare professional.
