How Percentages Work

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. For example, 25% means 25 out of every 100, which is equivalent to 0.25.

Percentage Formulas

Percentage of a number: (percentage ÷ 100) × number

What percent one number is of another: (part ÷ whole) × 100

Percentage change: ((new value − original value) ÷ |original value|) × 100

Examples

20% of 150 is 30. If 30 is compared with 150, then 30 is 20% of 150. If a value increases from 100 to 125, the percentage increase is 25%.

Common Uses

Percentage calculations are commonly used for discounts, taxes, tips, grades, financial changes, business metrics, statistics and everyday comparisons.
