How the Currency Converter Works

The converter multiplies the amount entered by the exchange rate for the selected currency pair. For example, if 1 USD equals 0.90 EUR, then 100 USD equals 90 EUR.

Are Exchange Rates Live?

Rates are retrieved automatically from an external currency data service that is updated daily. They are reference rates, not guaranteed transaction prices or continuously streaming market quotes.

Why Might Bank Rates Differ?

Banks, credit card companies and money transfer services may add exchange-rate margins, commissions or transaction fees. The actual amount received may therefore differ from this estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert Nigerian naira?

Yes, NGN is included when available from the exchange-rate provider, along with major currencies such as USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, INR and JPY.

Can I enter my own exchange rate?

Yes. Enable the manual exchange-rate option to calculate using a rate from your bank or another source.

Is this suitable for financial transactions?

This calculator is intended for general informational and educational purposes. Confirm actual rates and fees with your financial provider before making transactions.
