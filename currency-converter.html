How Currency Conversion Works

Currency conversion compares the value of one currency with another. Exchange rates change over time because of financial markets, monetary policy, economic conditions, and other factors.

Currency Conversion Formula

Converted Amount = Original Amount × Exchange Rate

For example, if 1 US dollar equals 0.90 euros, converting $100 at an exchange rate of 0.90 produces €90.

Why Your Actual Exchange May Be Different

Banks, credit card companies, currency exchanges, and other financial services may use different exchange rates and may charge transaction, conversion, or service fees. Therefore, the amount you actually receive may differ from this calculator's estimate.

Common Uses

A currency converter can be useful when planning international travel, comparing overseas purchases, estimating business expenses, reviewing international transactions, or converting prices between currencies.
